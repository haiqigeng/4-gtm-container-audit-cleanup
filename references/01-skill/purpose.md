# Product Purpose

`gtm-container-audit-optimize` performs a static, web-container-only expert audit of
one complete Google Tag Manager web container. It identifies defects,
materially non-optimal configurations, justified design, owner decisions, and
evidence limits; proposes a coherent greenfield-quality target and validates its
implemented static checks; and delivers one
trustworthy analyst workbook backed by a sealed canonical record.

The North Star is defined in `references/03-rules/audit-coverage.md`. Audit
quality, independent judgment, and trustworthy evidence boundaries take priority
over speed. Speed improvements are accepted only when they preserve those
properties.

One product execution comprises one comprehensive source scan and fresh-agent
assurance, two complete independent semantic audits in parallel, one fresh-agent
reconciliation, deterministic validation of the combined recommendations, and
workbook validation. Source-visible consequential cleanup belongs in the initial
audits. Simulated targets do not receive semantic review rounds, and completion
does not require convergence or promise that no further optimisation exists.
Separately authorized development evaluations may repeat outside that execution.
